8. Cheat sheet

  • ASCII is only 0–127. Anything above that is not an English letter, even if it looks like one.
  • NFKC normalization does not fix homoglyphs. Cyrillic о is not a variant of Latin o; it is a different letter that happens to look the same. Use the Unicode Confusables table (UTR#39).
  • Zero-width characters are invisible. U+200B, U+200C, U+200D, U+FEFF split words without showing up. "time" becomes t\u200bi\u200bm\u200be and your regex fails.
  • Check both the raw and the normalized string. Send the normalized version to the model, keep the raw version for audit.
  • Watch the transport layer. SMS is GSM-7 by default. Any Cyrillic or zero-width character forces UCS-2, which halves the segment size. That is a signal you get for free.
  • Do not treat "non-ASCII" as guilty. José, Müller, 東京, Arabic diacritics — all legitimate. Guilt comes from mixed script + 100% regular substitution.
